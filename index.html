How it was graded

Every pick in the chat was matched to its fixture and checked against the final score. Rankings use pick win rate: winning picks divided by picks that settled as a win or a loss. Voids and unknowns are left out of the rate.

Rules used

  • A coupon is one weekend or round. Picks posted over a day or two (usually Friday night and Saturday morning) are grouped together, and each player's final pick counts if they changed it.
  • A pick to win is graded on the 90-minute result. Draws lose. Wins on penalties or in extra time count as a loss.
  • Where bet365 paid a leg out early (2 goals up), it counts as a win, because that is how the coupon settled.
  • BTTS and draw picks are graded on that market.
  • Weeks where picks were posted but the coupon never went on are left out entirely.
  • Cado's own 8-fold on 3 Feb 2024 counts in the money totals but not in anyone's individual record.
  • On 27 Feb 2021 two coupons went on. Both count in the money, but each pick is only counted once.
  • Burst means you were the only losing leg on a coupon that was otherwise in.
  • Turns counts how often a player put the coupon on, and how many of those landed or were cashed out.

Money

Stake is £16 per coupon. Returns are only counted where the chat gives an amount, either a payout or a cash-out. Some of these are approximate, e.g. "about 240 each" on 21 Mar 2026 is taken as £1,920.

Gaps and judgement calls

    The chat runs from January 2021 to 17 October 2026. There were no coupons from August 2022 to July 2023, so 2022-23 is not shown.
